### About me

I have done [B.Tech in Computer Science (Data Science)](https://www.upes.ac.in/) from the University of Petroleum and Energy Studies, in 2026. My research interests lie at the intersection of **computer vision**, **deep learning**, and **generative modeling** — with a focus on 3D perception, saliency-aware architectures, and multimodal reasoning.

I am currently a Computer Vision Research Assistant at the [CVPR Unit, Indian Statistical Institute (ISI) Calcutta](https://www.isical.ac.in/), under the supervision of Prof. Ujjwal Bhattacharya, working on 4D world model and 3D computer vision in Adas system with Sudip Das from Valeo Brain (PI).
